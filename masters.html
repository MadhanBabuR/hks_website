Our masters

The founder of the Sangham and the Gurukkals and teachers who carry his lineage today. Gurukkal is the title given to a master of the Kalari.

Veerasree Sami Gurukkal

Founder (1934–2006)

Initiated into Kalaripayattu at seven, he went on to learn Ayurveda and the Tantra shastras and remained a student all his life.

He founded Hindustan Kalari Sangham in 1952 and treated patients who came from across India. His remembrance, Gurusmriti, is observed every May.

Teaching today

Lakshmanan Gurukkal

Acharya

Founded Kalarigram near Auroville in 2010 and leads the work on the Guru Veera Sri Sami Gurukkal Ayurveda Gurukulam in Calicut.

Shatrughnan Gurukkal

Gurukkal

Leads training and Marma Chikitsa at the Sangham, and teaches visiting students from abroad.

Radhika Gurukkal

Gurukkal
