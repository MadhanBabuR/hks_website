# Design brief: Phase 1 redesign pass

Self-authored under explicit creative delegation. Madhan, 26 Oct 2026: "deep dive into the design factors and have a very clean and structured and also beautiful while we scroll". Content stays as it is (content pack section B); this pass changes typography, layout and scroll behaviour only.

## What was wrong (from the review)
- Rozha One reads loud and dated, not classy or modern.
- Rounded cards, soft shadows and tiles everywhere: the page looked assembled from a kit.
- Every section was static; scrolling gave nothing back.

## Direction
- **Vibe:** a quiet temple, lamp-lit, exact. Calm authority, not spectacle.
- **References (outside the web):** a museum monograph on a master craftsman; the hush before a Kalari salutation; low oil-lamp light on laterite.
- **Range:** premium editorial. Sharp corners, hairlines, big photographs, generous space.
- **Structure:** distinct scenes, not one continuous world.
- **Assets:** HKS's own reels and photographs. Nothing generated.

## Type
- Display: **Instrument Serif** (one weight plus italic). It's a fine, high-contrast serif that reads as heritage without the weight of a poster face. Used large, tight, never bold.
- Text: **Geist**, a precise modern sans for body, navigation and controls.
- Two families, no third.

| 4 Ways in | curiosity | four doors slide past sideways: train, heal, stage, lineage | horizontal pan |
| 5 Brand words | conviction | Lineage, Discipline, Healing slide in from alternating sides over the pit | kinetic type over parallax image |
| 6 The year | belonging | months light up one by one beside a pinned heading | sticky side column with row reveal |
| 7 Close | invitation | the emblem settles, one action: WhatsApp | scale-in, then holds |

**The peak:** "the old photo of the founder slowly comes to life as you scroll." It lives in act 3, and act 2 before it stays quiet.

**Tell-someone sentence:** It's the site where the founder's photograph lights up like a lamp as you read his story.

**Authored silence:** the space between the statement and the founder scene is deliberate breathing room, not dead scroll.

## Inner pages
Shared, quieter devices: a parallax header image with the title rising in, image wipes as photographs enter, the line of Gurus drawing itself, and timeline rows lighting up in turn.

## Motion rules
`transform`, `opacity` and `clip-path` only. Reduced motion shows the final composed state with no pinning. Video pauses when off screen.
