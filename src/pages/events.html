Through the year

  • March

    This month

    International Women’s Day workshop

  • April

    This month

    Summer Kalari camp for children

  • May

    This month

    Gurusmriti

    The annual remembrance of Veerasree Sami Gurukkal.

  • June

    This month

    International Yoga Day workshop

  • July

    This month

    Guru Purnima Guru Pooja

  • Karkidakam

    This season

    Ganapathi Homam and Ramayana month poojas

    Karkidakam is the Malayalam month that runs from July to August.

  • August

    This month

    Independence Day and Onam

  • Navratri

    This season

    Golu, chanting and cultural evenings at the Kalari

    Golu is the festival display of dolls kept through the nine nights.

  • Vijayadashami

    This season

    Vidyarambham, when new students begin their training

    Vidyarambham means the beginning of learning.
