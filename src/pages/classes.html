Step by step, in the lineage’s way

Kalaripayattu is learnt step by step in the lineage’s own way: from the opening salutation and body conditioning to stances, steps, kicks and, in time, weapons.

New batches traditionally begin on Vijayadashami with Vidyarambham, the initiation that marks the beginning of a student’s learning.

Kalari classes

Morning and evening batches in the Kalari pit at Puthiyara, with separate evening slots for women and children.

Online classes

Live online sessions for beginners anywhere in the world, building a foundation in body sequences, leg swings and stances, with continuing batches for students who progress.

Workshops and camps

A summer Kalari camp for children every April, workshops for women, and short courses in weapon work such as Vadiveesal, the art of rotating the stick.

Open to everyone

No martial-arts background needed. Message us on WhatsApp for current timings and fees.
