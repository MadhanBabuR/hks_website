Performances

Kalaripayattu as an offering

Our students and Gurukkals carry Kalaripayattu to stages across India as an offering to the lineage.

Veera Vaibhavam: The Journey of a Warrior

Our signature showcase of unarmed forms and weapon combat, staged at the Sangham during Navratri and at the Mahamagha Kumbhamela on the banks of the Nila.

BRICS Demonstration Games, Ahmedabad (2026)

Together with Kalarigram, the Sangham represented India with a Kalaripayattu demonstration, broadcast live on Doordarshan Sports.

Gurusmriti Showcase

Every May, students of Hindustan Kalari Sangham and Kalarigram perform together in tribute to Veerasree Sami Gurukkal.
