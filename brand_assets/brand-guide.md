# Hindustan Kalari Sangham: brand guide (Phase 1)

A working guide for the website, drawn from the content pack (`content/hks-content-pack.md`).
The real logo is not here yet. When it arrives, add it to this folder and it replaces the
text wordmark and the placeholder mark everywhere.

## The idea

The site is laid out like the floor of a Kalari: a board of solid tiles set with narrow 8px
joints, in the colours of the earthen pit, the lime-plastered wall, oxblood and lamp gold. The
one recurring motif is the **Poothara**, the seven-tiered platform found in the corner of a
traditional Kalari, drawn as seven stacked bars.

## Colour

| Role | Name | Hex | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ground | Lime plaster | `#e6dfd9` | Page background |
| Raised tile | Paper | `#f1ede9` | Text tiles on the ground |
| Step | Surface | `#dbd1c9` | Alternate bands, the navigation tile |
| Ink | Deep maroon | `#3a0a0e` | Body text and headings on light tiles |
| Soft ink | Muted | `#6b4a45` | Secondary notes only |
| Field | Oxblood | `#7d0c14` | The mark tile, colour fields, primary buttons on light |
| Field | Earth | `#1f120e` | The pit: dark fields and the header action |
| Accent | Lamp gold | `#e0a23b` | Buttons on dark fields, the WhatsApp dock, the Poothara |
| On dark | Light | `#efe6da` | Text on oxblood and earth |

Oxblood and earth fields carry a faint lamp-glow (layered radial gradients) and the whole page
has a fine grain, so flat colour reads as plaster and earth rather than screen colour.

## Type

- **Literata** (Google Fonts), weight 500, for headings and display. Tight tracking (-0.03em) on large sizes.
- **Hanken Grotesk** (Google Fonts), 400–600, for body text, navigation and buttons. Line height 1.7.

Headings are in sentence case. No all-caps labels.

## Mark and wordmark

- Placeholder mark: `hks-mark.svg` (gold Poothara on oxblood). Used as the favicon.
- Wordmark: "Hindustan Kalari Sangham" set in Literata beside the mark.
- The real emblem (black-and-white, circular, two horses or warriors, the name in a ring)
  should replace both once the file is supplied, ideally as SVG.

## Voice

Devotional and reverent for the lineage, energetic for classes and performances, warm for
community events. Plain English; Malayalam terms are explained the first time they appear.
Brand words: Lineage, Discipline, Healing.

## Photographs

All photographs get the same treatment on the site: a light oxblood multiply layer and a dark
gradient at the bottom for captions. See `images/README.md` for the photos still needed.
