### About & Lineage
- **Intro:** Hindustan Kalari Sangham began in 1952, when a young Kalari practitioner from Calicut named Swaminathan opened a Kalari at Puthiyara. Initiated into Kalaripayattu at seven, and forced by family circumstances to leave school and work in a tile factory, he never let go of his learning, going on to study Ayurveda and the Tantra shastras. Known to generations as Veerasree Sami Gurukkal, he treated patients from across India and grew the Sangham into branches throughout Calicut district and beyond. Since his passing in 2006, his students and family have carried the lineage forward through daily training, healing and the annual Gurusmriti in his memory.
- **Lineage line:** Kottakkal Kanaran Gurukkal → Guru Govindan Kuttynambiar → Veerasree Sami Gurukkal
- **Milestones:**

  - 2010: Lakshmanan Gurukkal founds Kalarigram near Auroville
  - 2022: Online Kalaripayattu classes begin
